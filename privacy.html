Optional analytics

If you accept, we use Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics to understand visits to our public pages and interest in the organiser. Google Analytics may set first-party cookies such as _ga to distinguish visits. Our Google Analytics cookies are set for up to 180 days. Vercel Web Analytics provides aggregate page reports without analytics cookies.

We send the public page address and a general page title. Google Analytics can receive the referring website's origin, device/browser information and basic visit information. We remove query strings and fragments from the page addresses we send, and do not send names, email addresses, form contents, order details or checkout contents as analytics events. We do not enable advertising features or personalised ads through this setup.

Our named interest events record clicks to the organiser section and the fallback checkout link. They do not prove a purchase or measure completed orders. Checkout and order records remain in Shopify.

Google and Vercel process analytics information for us. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Vercel's Privacy Policy. Declining analytics prevents these optional analytics scripts loading.

Your choices

Accepting analytics is optional. Accept and reject are both available in our analytics panel. We remember your choice in this browser for up to 180 days using a local preference; it is not a marketing profile. If browser storage is unavailable, we may be unable to remember the choice.

Use Cookie settings in the footer to change your choice at any time. Rejecting after accepting stops future optional tracking, clears the Google Analytics cookies available to this website and reloads the page to unload the analytics scripts. It does not erase information already included in earlier reports.

You can also manage cookies and site data in your browser. Clearing the saved preference means we will ask again. Without JavaScript, optional analytics and the choice panel do not run.

Shopping and enquiries

The Shopify Buy Button and checkout provide the basket, payment and order services needed when you choose to shop. Those services have their own privacy and cookie handling. See the Shopify checkout and order privacy policy and our shipping and returns information.

The website also requests resources needed to display it, including fonts. Rejecting optional analytics does not prevent these ordinary website or shopping requests. If you email us, we use the information you provide to answer your enquiry; doing so does not subscribe you to marketing.
