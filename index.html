Founder

Built from
experience.

I’m Lewis, an ultra runner and adventurer who got tired of digging through kit bags when time mattered most.

After years of race weekends, airports, mountains and camper van trips across the UK, Europe and Africa, the same problem kept showing up: shoes in one place, nutrition in another, electronics buried somewhere else, and the important things never where they needed to be. From Ultra X events in Rwanda, Tanzania and Morocco to Scotland, England, Wales, Madeira, Mullardoch 12 and Castle to Castle, MYVICI was shaped by real kit chaos before real challenges.

Built from the journey

Made for people who actually move.

MYVICI was created to bring order to the chaos before the start line, the airport gate, the mountain car park and the early morning drive. It is storage for people who do not stand still.

What does MYVICI mean?

My
Vee-Chee.

MYVICI is pronounced My Vee-Chee. The name is inspired by Vici from the Latin phrase Veni, Vidi, Vici — “I came, I saw, I conquered.”

For us, it is not about beating someone else. It is about preparation, discipline and showing up ready for the journey ahead.
