Founder Story

Built from
experience.

I’m Lewis, an ultra runner and adventurer who got fed up digging through kit bags when time mattered most.

Race weekends, airports, mountain car parks and camper trips kept creating the same problem: shoes in one place, nutrition somewhere else, electronics buried under everything and the important bits never where they should be. MYVICI started as the answer to that problem and is now growing into a wider performance gear brand.

MYVICI is pronounced My Vee-Chee. The name is inspired by Vici from the Latin phrase Veni, Vidi, Vici — “I came, I saw, I conquered.”

For MYVICI, that is not about beating someone else. It is about preparation, discipline and showing up ready for your own challenge.
