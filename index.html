Built from the journey

Made for people who actually move.

After years of race weekends, airports, mountains and camper van trips, the same problem kept showing up: the gear that mattered most was always buried somewhere else. Shoes in one place. Nutrition in another. Electronics at the bottom of a bag. MYVICI was created to bring order to the chaos before the start line.

What does MYVICI mean?

My
Vee-Chee.

MYVICI is pronounced My Vee-Chee. The name is inspired by Vici from the Latin phrase Veni, Vidi, Vici — “I came, I saw, I conquered.”

For us, it is not about beating someone else. It is about preparation, discipline and showing up ready for the journey ahead.
