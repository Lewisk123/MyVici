Founder story

It started with
real kit chaos.

I’m Lewis, an ultra runner and adventurer. Race weekends, airports, mountain car parks and camper trips kept creating the same problem: shoes in one place, nutrition somewhere else, electronics buried underneath everything and the important bits never where they should be.

After enough long-distance events, travel days and stupidly early starts, the answer became obvious. The goal was not to create another piece of gear for the sake of having a product. It was to build something practical enough to earn its place every time the kit gets packed.

That first organiser became the start of MYVICI — a wider performance brand built around preparation, movement and the journey before, during and after the challenge.

What does MYVICI mean?
VENI.
VIDI.
VICI.

The name takes its inspiration from the famous phrase commonly translated as “I came, I saw, I conquered.” MYVICI turns that mindset inward: prepare properly, show up ready and conquer your own challenge.

01 / PrepareReady before you need it.The challenge gets easier when the important things are organised before the pressure starts.
02 / MoveGear should help, not distract.Products should remove friction and let you get on with running, travelling and doing the thing you packed for.
03 / ConquerYour challenge. Your victory.MYVICI is not about beating somebody else. It is about being prepared enough to face whatever you chose to take on.
